The Game

Bloody Claire is a psychological horror RPG focused on exploration, characters, story and turn-based encounters.

Story

Lara's life takes an unexpected turn when her best friend, Claire, disappears. As familiar places begin to feel strange and dreams start bleeding into reality, Lara sets out to find the truth.

Some questions are better left unanswered. Some dreams refuse to end.

What to expect

  • A story-driven adventure set around Morrow City.
  • Turn-based encounters.
  • Original music and visual assets.
  • A world with secrets to discover.

This page avoids major story spoilers. More details will be revealed as development progresses.
