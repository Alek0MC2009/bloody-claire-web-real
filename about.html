About Bloody Claire

Who we are

Bloody Claire is an independent videogame project created by alek0dev, a solo developer interested in videogames, programming, visual design and music.

The project brings together game development, original artwork, sound and music under one identity.

Our mission

The goal is to create a memorable story-driven RPG with its own atmosphere, characters and world, while keeping the experience accessible to players with a wide range of computers.

History

Bloody Claire grew out of earlier experiments in RPG development and evolved into a larger, independently developed project made with GameMaker.
